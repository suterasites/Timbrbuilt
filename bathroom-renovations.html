FAQ

Bathroom renovations, answered.

Something else? Call 0484 698 553 or send a message.

How much does a bathroom renovation cost?

It depends on the size of the room, the fixtures and tiles you choose, and how much has to change behind the walls. Rather than guess, we give you a fixed written quote after a free on-site measure, so the price you see is the price you pay.

How long does a bathroom renovation take?

Most full bathroom renovations take a few weeks on site once we start, depending on the size of the room, the finishes and how much needs to change behind the walls. You will get a realistic timeline with your quote.

Do you handle the waterproofing, plumbing and tiling?

Yes. We coordinate the plumber, waterproofer, tiler and electrician, with waterproofing done to the Australian Standard for wet areas. You deal with one point of contact, and the trades turn up in the right order so nothing gets missed between them.

Do you renovate older or heritage bathrooms?

Yes, and a lot of our work is exactly that. Older homes often hide surprises once the old bathroom comes out, like tired plumbing, movement or waterproofing that has failed. We deal with what we find and put it back properly, so the new bathroom is sound.

Do you build the vanity and joinery yourselves?

Yes. Timbr Built is a carpentry business first, so we build vanities, mirror cabinets, shaving cabinets and storage in-house to suit your space, rather than squeezing in an off-the-shelf unit that does not quite fit.

Which areas do you cover?

We renovate bathrooms across Melbourne's inner west, Hobsons Bay and the Wyndham growth corridor, including Footscray, Yarraville, Williamstown, Newport, Altona, Point Cook and the surrounding suburbs. If your suburb is not listed, get in touch and we will let you know.
